## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, welcoming, and technologically modern visual language for the institutional portal and internship hub of Faculdade de Minas (FAMINAS). Catering to students, faculty, partner enterprises, and administrative coordinators, the interface strikes a balance between academic gravitas and frictionless digital utility.

The design philosophy adopts a refined **Corporate / Modern** aesthetic enriched with structural depth and clear typographic cadence:
- **Academic Reliability:** Deep navy and structured surfaces convey legacy, institutional prestige, and educational rigor.
- **Dynamic Progression:** Vivid cobalt and cyan accents reflect professional opportunity, modern healthcare and technology programs, and career agility.
- **Clarity & Accessibility:** High-contrast tokens, tactile form controls, and crisp hierarchical framing prioritize readability under Brazilian accessibility standards (e-MAG / WCAG 2.1 AA).

## Colors

The palette balances institutional solemnity with purposeful digital feedback:

- **Primary (`#0B1B3D`):** Deep academic navy anchoring top-level navigation, structural headers, primary text, and dominant interactive actions.
- **Secondary (`#0066CC`):** Crisp institutional cobalt directing actionable UI, active links, focused navigation states, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber accent reserved for internship status flags, deadlines, notification indicators, and high-priority alerts.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool slate gray providing accessible supporting copy, subtle borders, and balanced icon colorations.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `accent-cyan` (`#0284C7`) and `accent-sky` (`#38BDF8`): Interactive highlights, progress tracks, and dynamic category pill accents.
  - `surface-page` (`#F8FAFC`) and `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`): Light architectural layers preventing screen glare while maintaining crisp separation.
  - `success-green` (`#16A34A`) & `danger-red` (`#DC2626`): Standardized academic validation for internship approvals, pending contracts, and document rejections.

## Typography

The typographic pairing unites **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers with **Inter** for technical body and administrative tables:

- **Display & Section Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric clarity with soft humanist qualities makes high-level announcements and portal landing sections feel approachable and contemporary.
- **Body & Data Density (Inter):** Highly legible x-height and neutral geometry ensure that technical documentation, internship contracts (TCE), compliance checklists, and contact directories remain effortlessly scannable across all devices.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-weight, slightly tracked tokens (`label-md`, `label-sm`) empower badges, status pills, and form field anchors with distinct legibility against complex layout backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal leverages a responsive 12-column grid system designed to accommodate dense institutional portals, student dashboard widgets, and split document views:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas spans `1280px` centered to optimize line lengths in academic text.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters; secondary navigation collapses into accessible sheet panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters; multi-column dashboard cards stack linearly into full-width card streams.
- **Vertical Spacing Scale:** Layout distances follow strict multiples of 4px/8px, using `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal input/badge padding, `space-md` (16px) for item gaps, `space-lg` (24px) for card body padding, and `space-xl` (40px) for section separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **ambient shadows combined with hairline structural borders** over layered tonal surfaces:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - `Level 0 (Canvas):` `#F8FAFC` page background.
  - `Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels):` Pure `#FFFFFF` surface framed by a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow (`0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.05)`).
  - `Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Dropdowns):` Elevated card elevation with a targeted navy-tinted shadow (`0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.03)`).
  - `Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Nav):` Pure `#FFFFFF` floating surface flanked by a pronounced backdrop shadow (`0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.08)`).
- **Edge Definition:** Shadows are never used alone; they are always accompanied by low-contrast borders (`#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1`) to retain sharp definition on varied monitor calibrations.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded Level 2** geometry:
- Standard elements (inputs, badges, chips, standard buttons) feature `0.5rem` (8px) border radius.
- Structural layout containers (document cards, directory panels, alerts) scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Small tags and status indicators use fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`) to contrast against the architectural rectangularity of cards and data grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep navy background (`#0B1B3D`), white text, `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding, `label-lg` typography. On hover: shifts to secondary cobalt (`#0066CC`) with subtle elevation lift.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with 1.5px border in `#0066CC`, text in `#0066CC`. On hover: subtle `#0066CC`/8% background wash.
- **Tertiary / Accent Action:** Solid `#F59E0B` with white or `#0B1B3D` high-contrast text, reserved for prominent CTAs such as "Inscreva-se" or "Enviar Relatório de Estágio".

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height `1.5rem` to `1.75rem`, rounded-full (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Uses semantic tint pairings:
  - *Em Andamento / Em Análise:* Amber wash (`#FEF3C7`) with text `#B45309`.
  - *Aprovado / Ativo:* Emerald wash (`#DCFCE7`) with text `#15803D`.
  - *Pendente / Documentação:* Sky wash (`#E0F2FE`) with text `#0369A1`.
  - *Indeferido / Encerrado:* Crimson wash (`#FEE2E2`) with text `#B91C1C`.

### Cards & Internship Listings
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Internal layout structured via `space-lg` padding, header block with category chip and institution icon, clear metadata grid (workload, stipend, location), and dedicated footer actions.
- Hover state: border transitions to `#93C5FD` with Level 2 elevation.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`, border-radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`, font `body-md`.
- Focus state: border `#0066CC` with a 3px ring of `#0066CC`/15%.
- Error state: border `#DC2626` with red validation helper text below.

### Alert Banners
- Border-radius `0.75rem`, border-left `4px solid`, background tinted to corresponding semantic tone (e.g., `#EFF6FF` with `#0066CC` border for informational academic notices; `#FFFBEB` with `#F59E0B` border for pending internship deadline warnings).

### Contact & Unit Directory Grid
- Modular 2-column or 3-column cards featuring unit location pins, office hours, direct WhatsApp/email triggers, and department supervisor indicators formatted in clean tabular hierarchy.
